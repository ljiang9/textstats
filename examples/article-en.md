# Notes on Writing with AI

Over the past year, AI writing tools have changed dramatically. What started as simple autocomplete has grown into structured long-form generation.

I use an AI assistant every day to draft outlines. The workflow is simple: list the key points, let the model expand them, then revise by hand.

But no tool replaces human judgment. Models often fabricate data with complete confidence, and cited papers may not exist at all.

So my rule is simple: verify every fact. Every number and every quotation must be traced back to its original source.

Interestingly, rewriting is where models shine most. Turning a wordy paragraph into something crisp and punchy is something they do better than most people.

Writing is thinking. Tools only accelerate the path from thought to text; they cannot think for you.

Beginners should start with short texts. Let the AI polish your emails first, then try weekly reports, and only then attempt long articles.

Finally, keep your API key safe and never commit it to a public repository.
